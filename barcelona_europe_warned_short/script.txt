Europe should be afraid of Barcelona again.

They haven't started this season. They've detonated it.

Six league games. Six wins. Twenty-eight goals.

Five against Elche. Five against Rayo. Five at Valencia. Four at Levante. Then seven against Racing.

And when Europe arrived, Barcelona smashed Feyenoord five-one.

This isn't one superstar carrying them. Raphinha scored five league goals in August, then hit a hat-trick against Racing. Lamine Yamal can create something from nothing. Pedri controls the speed of every attack. And the depth keeps the pressure coming after the substitutions.

Yes, they can still leave space at the back. But when a team scores in waves, from every position, one mistake against them becomes three goals in ten minutes.

Barcelona are not rebuilding anymore.

Europe has officially been warned.
