## Editing Style

- Change visuals frequently, roughly every sentence or two.
- Avoid leaving a training clip on screen longer than 8-10 seconds unless it is being used as background under a graphic.
- Crop, punch in, speed-ramp, or add a very light zoom to make repeated training footage feel fresh.
- Keep all B-roll muted unless the editor deliberately turns one into an insert moment.
