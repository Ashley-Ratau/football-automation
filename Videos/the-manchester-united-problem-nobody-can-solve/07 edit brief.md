# Edit brief

- Seven narration chapters, three clean inserts, target 7–9 minutes.
- First expert insert before 45 seconds. Inserts replace narration and receive no captions, mirroring, blur or music treatment.
- Narration shots average 3.5–5.5 seconds. Map each spoken mechanism to current press/training footage or an action-specific diagram.
- Selectively mirror live-action narration B-roll only. Never mirror diagrams or inserts.
- Apply subtle, feathered, time-limited logo blur only where a source mark is visible.
- Captions: single-line, narration only, up to four words, white Arial Bold 54 with black outline; chapter pivots and key evidence only.
- Prefer official club press/training and original diagrams. Avoid match broadcast footage except short transformative pundit excerpts.
- QC the full timeline for repetition, mismatches, insert boundaries, audio continuity and caption overlaps; inspect every section frame and listen to every VO check file.
- Private upload only after H.264/AAC 1920x1080 validation.
