# Why Every Manchester United Rebuild Fails

## Thesis

Michael Carrick genuinely repaired Manchester United once: after arriving on 13 January 2026 he restored familiar roles, took 39 league points, and finished third. The opening five league matches of 2026/27 then produced only five points. Carrick's 4-2-3-1 creates compactness and lethal transitions, but the relapse exposed weak rest-defence, inconsistent collective running, and too few reliable answers when transition space disappears.

The institution point is deliberately nuanced. Carrick inherits remnants of competing eras, but the 2026 hierarchy also gave him a permanent deal and a major midfield rebuild. This is now Carrick and the football structure's shared problem: turning short-term clarity into principles that survive every game state.

## Viewer payoff

Distinguish a manager bounce from a sustainable model; understand why a formation cannot repair spacing, effort and recruitment by itself; see why another reset risks repeating the same cycle.
