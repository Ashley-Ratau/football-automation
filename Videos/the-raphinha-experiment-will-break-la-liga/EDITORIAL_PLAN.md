The central question: what changes when a winger's movement becomes the reference point of the entire attack?

## Structure awaiting verified insert selection

1. Hook, approximately 30–40 seconds: centre-backs prepare for a striker, then face a moving target. Establish the latest Raphinha scoring evidence and the question behind it.
2. Pundit insert one, approximately 45–60 seconds: establish the scale of the transformation, with the speaker and historical context accurately introduced.
3. Role change, approximately 70 seconds: central starting position versus static target man; explain movements as analysis rather than fabricated tracking evidence.
4. The defensive dilemma, approximately 65 seconds: follow the runner, hold the line, or protect the passing lane. Distinct diagrams for each choice and its consequences.
5. Pundit insert two, approximately 45–60 seconds: add an independent observation, then explain its relevance to the mechanism.
6. Latest match case study, approximately 65 seconds: use verified Sevilla details and distinguish the actual event from generic illustrations.
7. League implications, approximately 60 seconds: sustained points pressure and Madrid's defensive choices; acknowledge games played and uncertainty.
8. Pundit insert three, approximately 45–60 seconds, only if it adds a different substantive point.
9. Payoff, approximately 35–45 seconds: scoring streaks fluctuate; the repeatable problem is movement combined with multiple passing and wide threats.

Target narration 330–355 seconds and inserts 150–180 seconds. Adjust against measured audio, not word count alone.

- Begin with expressive Raphinha footage, not a prolonged title slate.
- Use short clips, generally 2–3.8 seconds, selected for the words being spoken.
- Give each tactical passage its own scenario. Highlight the Raphinha marker consistently; do not reuse a generic pass animation for unrelated claims.
- Mark schematic analysis as illustration in source notes. Never suggest diagrams reproduce measured match positions.
- Retain original pundit sound and image. Lower the music bed completely during inserts. No Liam audio under inserts.
- No burned-in narration captions; diagrams and chapter navigation support comprehension.
