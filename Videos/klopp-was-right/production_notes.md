# Klopp Was Right

A quote-driven documentary using the same pipeline and style as `wenger-warned-us`. It's built fresh: nothing comes from the older `klopp-warned-us-about-manchester-city` folder.

**Final file:** `final video/klopp-was-right.mp4`

## Angle (and why)
City's proven **financial** breaches run from 2009/10 to 2017/18, which is before Klopp's one-point title races (2019 and 2022). So the video does **not** claim those titles were stolen. Instead:
- From **December 2018 to February 2023** (35 charges), City were charged with failing to cooperate with the investigation, and the commission upheld three of the four breaches. Liverpool were top of the league in December 2018.
- Klopp spent those years describing the financial gap. He never accused City of cheating ("it's legal and everything is fine"). He refused to comment when the charges landed. He says now that he doesn't want trophies from a courtroom.

## Soundbites (all dated, Klopp's own voice unless noted)
- **Oct 2022**, HaytersTV, before Liverpool v City: "Nobody can compete with City... We cannot act like them... three clubs... can do what they want, financially. It's legal and everything is fine." Whisper heard "illegal"; the caption is corrected to "legal", which matches published reports.
- **May 2019**, after the 97-point season (Koora Shoot re-upload of the post-match): "97 points is incredible... in each other country it would have been easily enough."
- **Aug 2019**, Pep (HaytersTV): "Of course you bother me... it's not true" and "when something is wrong... will be punished."
- **Feb 2020** (Guardian): "I feel for Pep... in the end we all have to respect some rules."
- **Jul 2020** (This Is Anfield): "not a good day for football... FFP is a good idea... based on the right sources... I come from Germany... not an owner-based system... where you get the money from."
- **Aug 2021** (CGTN): "I'm never surprised about the financial power of Chelsea or City or United... they always find a solution to do these kinds of things."
- **May 2022** (Sky Sports): "If you want to win big, you have to be ready to lose big... we chased probably the best team in the world really to the wire."
- **Feb 2023:** Pep (Sky Sports): "we are already being condemned". Klopp (Sky Sports News): "I've absolutely nothing to say about it."
- **Jan 2025** (BeanymanSports): "just book a flight, I'd buy the beer... our own parade in my garden."
- **30 Sep 2026**, Germany press conference in German, HaytersTV with burned-in English subtitles: "it's not as if I feel I need to have a bus parade... I'm completely at peace with what happened and what we achieved. We battled it out on the pitch."

## Facts verified
- **Verdict:** PL statement, 29 Sep 2026. Sham contracts; more than £900m; seasons 2009/10–2017/18; "concerted efforts to stop and frustrate the investigation"; sanctions at a separate hearing. City appealed on 2 Oct 2026.
- **Failure-to-cooperate charges:** 35 charges covering Dec 2018–Feb 2023 (Football365, Blue News).
- **Title races:** 2018/19, City 98, Liverpool 97, with Liverpool losing one game. 2019/20, Liverpool champions by 18 points. 2021/22, City 93, Liverpool 92.
- **Klopp's career:** Dortmund Bundesliga titles in 2011 and 2012. Joined Liverpool in Oct 2015 and left in 2024. Appointed Germany head coach on 24 July 2026 (DFB).
- **Transfers:** Grealish was a British-record transfer to City in Aug 2021. Haaland joined City in summer 2022.

## YouTube description draft
On 29 September 2026, an independent commission found Manchester City guilty of using "sham" sponsorship contracts to inflate their finances by more than £900 million, and found that City made "concerted efforts to stop and frustrate" the Premier League's investigation, which began in December 2018.

Those were the years of Jürgen Klopp's title races with Pep Guardiola's City: 97 points that weren't enough, 92 points that weren't enough, and a manager who kept describing the gap in his own words. This video covers "three clubs in world football can do what they want, financially", "I'm never surprised about the financial power of City", his reaction to City's UEFA ban and its overturning, Pep's replies, and what Klopp said after the verdict.

City deny all wrongdoing and have lodged an appeal. Sanctions have not yet been decided, and the financial breaches found (2009/10–2017/18) came before Klopp's title races.

Every video here is researched, written and edited by me. AI is used for narration only.
