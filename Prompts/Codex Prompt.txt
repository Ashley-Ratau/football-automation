

You are helping me create a football YouTube essay video from scratch.

Your job is to handle the process in stages, and only move to the next stage after completing the current one clearly.

## 1. Research Stage
Start by researching the video topic deeply.

Your research should include:
- the main angle of the video
- the strongest narrative framing
- recent relevance or news ties if applicable
- the most important names, events, or controversies connected to the topic
- what kind of videos on this topic usually perform well
- what emotional angle is most likely to get clicks and retention

Also research possible insertion clips:
- find the most relevant clips that could be inserted into the video
- look for clips on YouTube, interviews, TV appearances, pundit reactions, social media, ads, or other public sources
- prioritize clips that are actually available and usable
- prefer clips that are meaningful enough to be inserted as full moments, not just background noise
- keep the number of insert clips limited to 5 or 6 maximum
- identify whether each clip is likely to work as a narrative insert or as B-roll

After the research, give me a short summary of:
- the best video angle
- the strongest insert clip opportunities
- any risks or weak points in the idea
- whether the topic is worth pursuing

## 2. Planning Stage
Once the research is done, create a full video plan.

The plan should show:
- the overall structure of the video
- the opening hook
- the sequence of sections
- where insert clips will go
- where B-roll will go
- how the story should build emotionally
- how the video should end

Important rules:
- the majority of the video should still be narration
- insert clips should be used strategically, not constantly
- each insert clip should happen at a natural handoff point
- the narration should lead naturally into the insert clip
- when the insert clip plays, the voiceover stops completely
- after the insert clip ends, the narration resumes naturally
- do not plan for voiceover to play softly under the insert clip

Present the plan in a clear, easy-to-read format.

## 3. Script Writing Stage
After the plan is approved, write the full script.

The script must:
- be written in sections
- match the planned structure exactly
- be paced for YouTube retention
- have a very strong first minute
- build tension and curiosity early
- feel natural and interesting, not robotic
- include clear handoff lines before insert clips
- end each section in a way that allows a clean transition into the next insert clip

Script rules:
- do not write one giant uninterrupted script
- divide it into sections
- each section should be suitable for one continuous voiceover block
- each section should naturally lead into a planned insert clip if one follows
- keep the flow smooth and story-driven
- make it compelling enough to hold attention throughout

After writing the script, provide:
- the full script
- a section-by-section breakdown
- where the insert clips go
- where B-roll should be used

## 4. Thumbnail Stage
After the script and plan are done, create 2 or 3 thumbnail concepts.

The thumbnails should:
- be based on the title, research, and script angle
- fit the style of successful football essay thumbnails
- be designed for clicks
- have strong visual contrast
- have a clear focal subject
- use faces and expressions effectively
- match the emotional tone of the video
- reflect the specific angle of the story

If I give you a style reference, follow that style closely.

Show me:
- 2 or 3 thumbnail directions
- a short explanation of each
- which one you recommend and why

## 5. Final Output Format
When you finish, give me these outputs in order:
1. research summary
2. video plan
3. full sectioned script
4. thumbnail concepts
5. any notes on insert clips and B-roll

Keep everything practical, specific, and ready for production.
